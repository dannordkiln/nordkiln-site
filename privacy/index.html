Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

NordKiln ("we") runs nordkiln.dev. This policy explains what we collect.

Early-access list

If you join the early-access list, we store your email address to tell you when NordKiln Scan launches and to send an occasional newsletter. The signup form is currently processed by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which forwards it to our email. You can unsubscribe or ask us to delete your address at any time by emailing dan.nordkiln@gmail.com.

Scans (when launched)

We will store the URL you scan, the findings and the account you used, only to provide the service. We do not sell data. Test-account credentials are used only during the scan and are not kept afterwards.

Analytics and cookies

This site currently sets no tracking cookies.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can request access to, correction of or deletion of your data. Contact dan.nordkiln@gmail.com. NordKiln is based in Norway.
