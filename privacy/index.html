Privacy

Last updated 2026-10-21.

Who is responsible?

NordKiln is responsible for the data described here. Contact: scan.nordkiln@mail.grokbot.com.

What we collect

What you enter in the forms: name, email, phone, dates, address or accommodation, and for owners location, gear, price and an optional photo.

Why

To answer your request, put renters and owners in touch, and create listings. The legal basis is your request to us (contract) and your consent.

Who receives it

Forms are sent via the FormSubmit service to our email inbox. When you request to rent, we share your name, contact details, dates and location with the owner of that gear. We never sell data.

Map

The map is loaded from OpenStreetMap and the map library from unpkg.com. They see your IP address when the map is shown. We use no tracking or analytics tools.

How long

We delete requests no later than 12 months after our last contact, unless accounting rules require otherwise.

Your rights

You can ask for access, correction or deletion by emailing scan.nordkiln@mail.grokbot.com. You can complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet).

Database, email and notifications

Listings, owner accounts and rental requests are stored with Supabase (servers in the EU). Email is sent via Resend. Push notifications go through your browser's push service (Apple, Google or Mozilla) and only contain the item and dates, never names or contact details. Rental requests are shared only with the owner of that gear. If card payment is enabled, payments are processed by Stripe.
