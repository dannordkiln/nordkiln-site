When to pick which

Choose Bleek if

You want a scan of the public site right now: headers, leaked keys, open files, and injection probes, without creating test users.

Choose NordKiln if

Your worry is "user B sees user A's data". That needs two logged-in accounts, which URL scanners do not do. Join early access and use the free Supabase check while the scanner opens.
