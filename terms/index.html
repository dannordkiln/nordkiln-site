What NordKiln is

NordKiln puts people who want to rent out gear (owners) in touch with people who want to rent it (renters). We don't own the gear, and the rental agreement is between owner and renter.

Prices and fee

The owner sets the rental price. The renter also pays a service fee of 15% of the rental price, minimum 49 NOK per booking. Owners pay no fee.

Request and confirmation

A request is not binding. The rental is agreed only once the owner has confirmed and you have received the total price and payment details from us by email.

Deposit, damage and insurance

The owner may ask for a deposit. Owner and renter check the gear at pickup and return and agree between themselves how any damage or loss is covered. NordKiln provides no insurance or payment guarantee.

Owner responsibilities

Owners must own the gear, describe it accurately and keep it in safe condition. We may decline or remove listings.

Renter responsibilities

Renters must use the gear with care and return it as agreed. Use of gear outdoors and in the mountains is at your own risk.
