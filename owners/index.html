Questions and answers

What does it cost?

You see the total in the booking before you confirm: rent, a small service fee and any delivery. Owners list for free.

What if something gets damaged?

Normal wear is free. Damage beyond that is paid at actual repair cost, up to current value, documented with photos at pickup and return. See the rental rules.

How do I pay?

You pay by Vipps or cash when you pick up the gear. Nothing is paid on the website.

Is there a deposit?

It's shown on the listing and in the booking. The deposit is paid by Vipps at pickup and refunded within 24 hours after a checked return.
